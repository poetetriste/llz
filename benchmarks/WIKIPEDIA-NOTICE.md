# Wikipedia benchmark corpus notice

`corpus.txt` contains plain-text extracts retrieved from the Russian-language
Wikipedia API on 2026-11-05. The included article titles are listed in
`manifest.json`.

Wikipedia text is available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
License. The repository's MIT license does not replace or supersede that
license for this benchmark corpus. Source: <https://ru.wikipedia.org/>.

The corpus is included because an exact byte stream is required to reproduce
compression results. Its SHA-256 digest and retrieval date are recorded in
`manifest.json`.
